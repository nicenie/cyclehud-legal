VeloLume · Privacy

What VeloLume does with your data

Effective 16 November 2026 · Applies to version 1.0 (1) and later

VeloLume turns your ride data and your video into one clip with a dashboard on top. Everything happens on your device: no account, no network requests, no analytics SDK. The FIT file and the video you import never leave it.

1. What we collect

Nothing to collect. The table below is the complete list of data the app touches, and all of it stays local:

DataWhat the app does with itUploadedUsed for tracking
Activity file (.fit) Read only after you pick it in the system file picker; parsed for time, speed, heart rate, altitude and similar fields to drive the dashboard No No
Video file Read only after you choose it; used for time calibration and compositing. A copy is kept in the app container so you can resume editing next launch No No
Exported clip On macOS it is written to the folder you choose in the save panel. On iOS it is written to the app's own Exports folder first, then you decide where it goes via the system share sheet (Files, Photos, AirDrop) No No
Unit preference and last task The unit preference lives in local preferences; the last-task record lives in the app container so the next launch can restore it No No

And nothing else

  • No sign-up, and no phone number, email address or account information collected
  • No ad SDK, no analytics SDK, no crash-reporting SDK
  • No location, contacts, microphone or camera permission is requested
  • No tracking across apps or websites. The privacy manifest shipped inside the app declares no tracking and no collected data types, which matches this section

2. Where your files live

  • All processing is local. Parsing the FIT file, compositing the video and writing the output all happen on your device. No step sends file contents anywhere.
  • Two things stay in the app container: a copy of the imported video (so you can resume next launch) and the last-task record. Both stay on this device.
  • A bookmark for reopening files. To reopen the files you picked last time, the app stores a system-issued security-scoped bookmark locally. It is a pointer to a file, not the file's contents, and it does not leave the device.
  • Deleting the app clears it. Removing the app deletes the container copy, the record and the bookmark. Clips you exported yourself are untouched — they are wherever you saved them.

3. Network use

This app makes no network requests, so there is no uploading, no cloud sync and no online templates. The macOS build ships without the network-client sandbox entitlement, and the iOS build has no networking code path at all.

If a future version adds a feature that needs the network, we will update the effective date at the top of this page first and state inside the app what was added and why.

4. Permissions and file access

Permission / entry pointPurposeIf you decline or avoid it
User-selected file read-write (macOS sandbox) Gives the app access to the FIT file, video and export folder you choose in the open/save panel — and to nothing else Import and export are unavailable; the rest of the app stays browsable
System photo picker (iOS) Hands over only the one video you select; the app cannot see the rest of your library Import the video from the Files app instead; no feature is lost

The app does not request location, contacts, microphone, camera or health data access.

5. Children

VeloLume is a general-purpose utility not aimed at children under 13. Because the app collects no data and makes no network requests, there is no path by which a child's personal information could be collected.

6. Changes to this policy

When this policy changes we update the effective date at the top. Changes that affect what is collected or how the network is used are announced inside the app.
